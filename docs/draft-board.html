How to read it. Every column is one manager’s team, top to bottom. The order snakes: the arrow beside each round number shows which way it runs, and the rail marks where that round starts. So round 1 goes Jerv → Calvin and round 2 comes straight back Calvin → Jerv, which is why the pick numbers in the corner of each cell count down across even rounds. Calvin and Jerv sit on the turns and pick twice in a row. Hover any cell for the alternatives.

What the model knows. Each manager’s positional schedule (when his second and third back arrive), how far ahead of market he opens at each position, the round he habitually takes a kicker, and two hand-entered facts — Johnny will not draft McCaffrey, and alex has Taylor over him. It does not know injuries, camp news, or anything that happened after today’s ADP pull.

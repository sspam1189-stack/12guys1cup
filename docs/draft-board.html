How to read it. Every column is one manager’s team, top to bottom. The order snakes: the arrow beside each round number shows which way it runs, and the rail marks where that round starts. So round 1 goes Jerv → Calvin and round 2 comes straight back Calvin → Jerv, which is why the pick numbers in the corner of each cell count down across even rounds. Calvin and Jerv sit on the turns and pick twice in a row. Hover any cell for the alternatives. Where no single player cleared 12% the cell shows how often that position went at that pick instead of naming somebody who turned up once. Hover for the most likely name anyway. Every column is also held to that manager’s own quota, so the board cannot hand him eight receivers just because eight separate cells each leaned that way.
